## 1. Gamma — Best Overall AI for PPT

Gamma doesn't just create slides — it generates complete, well-designed presentations from a single prompt. Type "Create a 10-slide deck about AI adoption in Indian manufacturing" and you get a polished deck with proper structure, relevant content, and design choices that actually look good.

**Why it's #1 for PPT:**
- Generates complete decks in under 90 seconds
- Design quality is noticeably better than competitors
- Exports directly to .pptx format for PowerPoint editing
- Free tier includes 10 AI credits (enough for 2-3 full presentations)

**Price in India:** Free tier available. Plus plan at $10/mo (≈₹930/mo) gives 400 credits. Pro at $20/mo (≈₹1,860/mo) for unlimited.

**The catch:** Free tier adds a Gamma watermark to exports. For classroom presentations displayed on screen, this is fine. For client-facing PPTs, you'll need Plus.

**Read full review:** [Gamma Review](/review/gamma)

## 2. Canva AI — Best When Design Matters More Than Content

Canva approaches presentations differently. Instead of generating content from scratch, it provides thousands of templates enhanced with AI features like Magic Write (text generation), Magic Design (layout suggestions), and Magic Eraser (image editing).

**Why it works for PPT:**
- Largest template library of any tool — thousands of India-relevant designs
- Magic Write generates slide text in context
- Exports to .pptx with formatting preserved
- Free tier is more generous than Gamma for manual design work

**Price in India:** Free tier with limited AI (5 uses/month). Pro at $12/mo (≈₹1,116/mo).

**Best for:** Users who want control over design and use AI as an assistant, not the primary creator.

**Read full review:** [Canva AI Review](/review/canva-ai)

## 3. Google Slides + Gemini — Best Free Option for PPT

If you have a Google account, you already have access to AI-powered presentations. Gemini integration in Google Slides lets you generate slide content, create speaker notes, and get layout suggestions — all for free.

**Why it's the best free choice:**
- Completely free, no credit limits
- Exports to .pptx for PowerPoint compatibility
- Works in your browser, no software to install
- Gemini generates content that's factually grounded

**Price in India:** Free with any Google account. Workspace plans add collaboration features.

**The limitation:** Design output is basic. Google Slides templates look like Google Slides templates — functional but not impressive. For a college presentation, fine. For a client pitch, use Gamma or Canva instead.

**Read full review:** [Google Slides + Gemini Review](/review/google-slides-gemini)

## 4. Beautiful.ai — Best for Corporate Consistency

Beautiful.ai enforces design rules automatically. Every slide follows your brand guidelines — colours, fonts, logo placement. The AI suggests content and layouts, but the real value is preventing design inconsistency across large team decks.

**Why corporate teams use it:**
- Brand enforcement at slide level
- Smart templates that auto-adjust layout
- Exports to .pptx
- Team collaboration features

**Price in India:** No free tier (discontinued). Pro at $12/mo (≈₹1,116/mo). Team at $40/mo (≈₹3,720/mo).

**The drawback:** No free tier means you're paying before you know if it fits your workflow. Gamma's free tier makes it a lower-risk starting point.

**Read full review:** [Beautiful.ai Review](/review/beautiful-ai)

## 5. PowerPoint + Copilot — For Microsoft Ecosystem Users Only

Microsoft's own AI add-on for PowerPoint. Copilot suggests content, refines design, generates speaker notes, and creates slides from Word documents. But the pricing makes sense only if you're already paying for Microsoft 365.

**Why it exists:**
- Works directly inside PowerPoint — no export/import
- Creates slides from Word documents and outlines
- Integration with OneDrive, Teams, SharePoint

**Price in India:** Copilot Pro at $20/mo (≈₹1,860/mo) on top of Microsoft 365 subscription. Total cost: ≈₹4,836/user/month for the full stack.

**The honest take:** At ≈₹4,836/month, this is 5x the price of Gamma for presentation AI that isn't 5x better. Only justifiable if your entire organization runs on Microsoft and you need native integration.

**Read full review:** [PowerPoint + Copilot Review](/review/powerpoint-copilot)

## FAQ

**Which AI tool exports to .pptx format?**
All five tools on this list export to .pptx (PowerPoint format). Gamma, Canva, and Beautiful.ai export directly. Google Slides exports via File → Download → .pptx.

**Can AI make a PPT from a PDF or Word document?**
Yes — PowerPoint Copilot can create slides directly from Word documents. Gamma can also accept pasted text or outlines and generate a complete deck from them.

**What's the best free AI for making PPT?**
Google Slides + Gemini is completely free with no limits. Gamma offers 10 free credits which is enough for 2-3 complete presentations. Both export to .pptx.

**Is Gamma better than PowerPoint Copilot?**
For pure slide generation quality, yes. Gamma produces better-designed slides faster and costs ≈₹930/mo versus Copilot's ≈₹1,860/mo (plus M365). Copilot's advantage is native Microsoft integration.

**Which AI tool is best for making PPT for college presentations in India?**
Gamma free tier or Google Slides + Gemini. Both are free, both export to .pptx, and Gamma's AI-generated designs look professional enough for academic presentations.
